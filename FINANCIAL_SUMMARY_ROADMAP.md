# Rugged App: Complete Itemized Financial Summary & Scaling Roadmap

This document details all revenues, store commissions (Google & Apple 15% cuts), annual developer/domain fees, and backend server scaling expenses for **Rugged (Affu Labs)** from **100 users** up to **1,000,000 users**.

---

## 1. Itemized List of All Fixed Costs & Third-Party Fees

### A. Store Platforms & Distribution Accounts
* **Google Play / Apple App Store Commission**: **15% of Gross Subscription Revenue** *(deducted automatically at checkout)*.
* **Apple Developer Program Annual Fee**: **$99.00 / year** (~**$8.25 / month**).
* **Google Play Developer Account**: **$25.00 one-time fee** (*$0.00 recurring*).

### B. Domain & Hosting
* **Custom Domain (`affulabs.com`)**: **~$15.00 / year** (~**$1.25 / month**).
* **Web Hosting (Landing Page & Deep Links)**: **$0.00 / month** *(GitHub/Cloudflare Pages Free Tier)*.

### C. Backend Infrastructure (Supabase & Resend)
* **At 100 to 10,000 Users**:
  * Supabase (Free Tier): **$0.00 / month**
  * Resend Email API (Free Tier): **$0.00 / month**
* **At 100,000 Users**:
  * Supabase Pro + Storage Overage: **$35.00 / month**
  * Resend Pro Plan: **$20.00 / month**
* **At 1,000,000 Users**:
  * Supabase Pro + Upgraded Compute Instance: **~$150.00 / month**
  * Resend Scale Plan: **~$60.00 / month**
  * CDN & Bandwidth: **~$50.00 / month**

### D. Pricing Structure
* **Monthly Pass**: **$6.00 / mo**
* **3-Month Pass**: **$16.00 / 3 mo** (*Save 11% vs Monthly | Effective $5.33/mo*)
* **6-Month Pass**: **$30.00 / 6 mo** (*Save 17% vs Monthly | Effective $5.00/mo*)
* **Annual Pass**: **$50.00 / yr** (*Save 31% vs Monthly | Effective $4.17/mo*)

## 3. Key Observations & Takeaways

1. **Google/Apple Cut (15%)**: At 1,000,000 users, Google and Apple take **$23,625.00/month**. However, because you retain **85% ($133,875.00/month from subs)**, your net income remains massive.
2. **Break-Even Threshold**: With **$50/yr** annual passes, you need only **19 annual subscribers** to cover all baseline fees plus **Supabase Pro** ($35/mo) and **Resend Pro** ($20/mo).
3. **99.8% Operating Margin**: Even after store cuts, Apple fee, domain, Supabase Pro compute, Resend Scale, and CDN bandwidth, your take-home net profit is **$162,555.50 / month** at 1M users.
